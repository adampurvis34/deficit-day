Loading your binder…

Your cards appear here as sleeved tiles with their current value.

  1. Tap Add photos and take a picture of a card, a few cards laid flat, a slab, or a whole set.
  2. Claude reads the player, year, set, card number, parallel and grade, and estimates a value in CAD.
  3. Check the details, fix anything it misread, and save.
  4. Come back and tap Update values whenever you want fresh estimates. Each one is added to the chart.

Values are Claude's estimates from recent sales it knows about, not live market quotes. For a card that matters, paste real sold listings into the card's page and Claude prices it from those.
